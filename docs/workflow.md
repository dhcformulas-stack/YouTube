# Video Production Workflow

This document outlines the pipeline for producing 30-minute documentary videos for Chronicle Forge.

## 1. Brief Ingestion
The process begins with a user-provided topic and set of criteria. This is typically retrieved from the shared database.

## 2. Research Phase
Deep research is conducted to ensure historical and scientific accuracy.
- **Tools:** `agent-browser` for web research.
- **Output:** A structured research document (`research.md`) containing verified facts, key dates, and sources.
- **Fact-Checking:** Initial verification of sources.

## 3. Scriptwriting Phase
A 30-minute documentary script is written based on the research.
- **Tone:** Engaging, educational, and narrative-driven.
- **Length:** 4,500 - 6,000 words (approx. 150-200 words per minute).
- **Structure:**
    - Hook (0-2 mins)
    - Introduction (2-5 mins)
    - Chapter 1: Foundations (5-12 mins)
    - Chapter 2: The Core Event/Principle (12-22 mins)
    - Chapter 3: Impact and Legacy (22-28 mins)
    - Conclusion and Call to Action (28-30 mins)
- **Visual Cues:** The script includes cues for image/video generation.

## 4. Visual Asset Generation
AI-generated images are created for each visual cue in the script.
- **Formats:** 16:9 images for video content, 1280x720 for thumbnails.
- **Consistency:** Maintain a consistent visual style throughout the video.

## 5. Narration Generation
The script is converted into a high-quality AI voiceover.
- **Voice Selection:** Professional, clear, and appropriate for the topic.

## 6. Video Assembly
The audio, images, and visual cues are compiled into a final video file.
- **Synchronization:** Ensure images match the narration cues.
- **Background Music:** Subtle, thematic music to enhance the atmosphere.

## 7. Final Fact-Check and Review
A final pass to ensure no errors were introduced during scriptwriting or assembly.
- **Checklist:**
    - Are all dates correct?
    - Are scientific terms used accurately?
    - Are visual representations appropriate?
    - Does the video meet the 30-minute length requirement?

## 8. Delivery
The final video and metadata (title, description, tags) are delivered to the customer.
